Terms of Service

Effective date: November 5, 2026

SimpleMail is an independent desktop email client maintained by TimohovAS for personal use and a small group of users. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Microsoft.

Your accounts and email

Use SimpleMail only with accounts you own or are authorized to access, and follow your email provider's terms. You control the recipients and content of messages sent from your accounts. Do not use the application for spam, unauthorized access or other unlawful activity.

Local files and backups

SimpleMail stores downloaded mail on your computer. You are responsible for protecting that computer and keeping appropriate backups. Email operations such as sending, moving and deleting messages may also affect your provider's servers and other devices connected to the same mailbox.

Availability

SimpleMail is an actively developed project. Availability of sign-in, sending and synchronization depends on your network, email provider, account settings and provider policies. Keep independent backups of important mail. The project does not provide a service-level commitment for support or availability.

Privacy and support

The Privacy Policy explains how the application accesses and stores data. Source code, release information and support are available in the SimpleMail GitHub repository. Do not post confidential account or message data in public issues.
